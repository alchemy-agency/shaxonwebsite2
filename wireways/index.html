WIREWAYS BY SHAXON

WIREWAY
PRODUCT LINE

Galvanized steel modular wireway systems for elevator shafts, mechanical rooms, and industrial infrastructure.

01

Wireway Trough with Cover

120″ modular trough in nine standard cross-sections. Ships with two 60″ covers, screws, and a factory-installed coupling.

VIEW PRODUCT
02

Wireway Trough without Cover

Open 120″ trough for field-customized configurations or separately ordered covers. Includes a factory-installed coupling.

VIEW PRODUCT
03

45° Elbow — Type C

Matched-size 45° directional fitting for gradual turns and angled routing in same-size wireway runs.

VIEW PRODUCT
04

45° Elbow — Type D

Stepped 45° elbow for shallow-angle transitions between two different wireway cross-sections.

VIEW PRODUCT
05

90° Elbow — Type C

Matched-size 90° elbow for clean right-angle turns in same-size galvanized steel wireway runs.

VIEW PRODUCT
06

90° Elbow — Type D

Stepped 90° elbow for right-angle transitions between two different wireway cross-sections.

VIEW PRODUCT
07

Wireway Coupling

Galvanized steel sleeve for securely joining two trough sections end-to-end in a straight wireway run.

VIEW PRODUCT
08

Wireway Cover, 60 Inches

Replacement or add-on galvanized steel cover available in widths that match each standard trough cross-section.

VIEW PRODUCT
09

Wireway End Plate

Galvanized terminal closure plate for sealing the open end of a trough section or wireway fitting.

VIEW PRODUCT
10

Wireway Panel Connector

Clean galvanized steel transition for attaching a wireway run directly to panels, cabinets, or enclosures.

VIEW PRODUCT
11

Wireway Reducer

Inline galvanized reducer for transitioning between different trough cross-sections in a straight run.

VIEW PRODUCT
12

Wireway Tee — Type C

Matched-size three-way tee for branching galvanized steel wireway runs at a clean 90° angle.

VIEW PRODUCT
13

Wireway Tee — Type D

Stepped three-way tee for branching a wireway run while transitioning into a different trough cross-section.
